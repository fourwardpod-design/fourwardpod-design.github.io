BootcampGuide: top of Google and cited by AI, with 20 leads in its first month.

BootcampGuide compares coding and professional courses in India. About five weeks after its first post, it was the top Google result for five comparison searches, cited in seven Google AI Overviews, and it brought in 20 leads in its first month.

Overview

BootcampGuide is an independent comparison site for coding and professional courses in India. It is our own site, not a client: we built it and write it, using the same search and AI visibility work we run for B2B SaaS teams.

Every listing shows the same facts, in the same shape: the fee in rupees, how long the course runs, the format, the next batch date, EMI and placement support. Students can compare courses side by side before they speak to anyone.

The challenge

A new domain, entering searches where the institutes' own pages, Reddit, Quora and YouTube take most of page one. Fees are often quoted "on request", so students struggle to compare like for like, and AI answers are only as good as the pages AI tools can find and trust.

What we did

Pages for the exact comparison

Head-to-head pages for the questions students ask: Scaler vs Masai, Masai School vs AccioJob, and upGrad vs Great Learning vs AlmaBetter vs AccioJob.

Data nobody else had published

A fee study read off 85 course pages at 16 institutes. Google's AI Overview for "coding bootcamp fees in india" now quotes its median fee of ₹85,000.

Pages AI can read and quote

Prerendered HTML, a robots.txt that welcomes search and AI crawlers, a named author on every post, and a short answer at the top of each page.

The same facts for every course

194 course pages and 16 institute pages in one format, each with a course-details form for students who are ready to talk to an institute.

Results

About five weeks after the first post, BootcampGuide had a page-one Google result for ten of the searches we checked, and was the top result for five. Google's AI Overview cited it for seven. In its first month, the site brought in 20 leads.

The takeaway

Comparison pages, original data and pages that AI tools can read did the work. A new site reached the top of Google and the sources inside AI Overviews within weeks of its first post.

It is the same work we run for B2B SaaS teams: comparison and alternatives pages for the questions buyers ask, data people want to quote, and pages that search engines and AI engines can read and cite.
