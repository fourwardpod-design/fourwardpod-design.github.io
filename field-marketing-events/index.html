Questions about events.

Why small events instead of a big booth?

A booth gives you a few minutes with strangers. A dinner gives you two hours with the buyers you chose. For considered B2B deals, the second moves pipeline further.

Can you fill the room with the right people?

That is the main job. We build the list with your sales team, invite by name and use paid media only to reach those accounts.

Which cities do you cover?

We plan events wherever your buyers work. The team has run them in cities across Asia and Europe, from Mumbai and Singapore to Berlin and Barcelona.

What happens after the event?

Every guest reaches sales within the week, with notes from the night. We also turn the evening into a recap, photos and clips your team can share.

Do you handle conferences and booths too?

Yes. If a conference is worth attending, we plan your presence around it, including side dinners and meetings, so the trip returns more than badge scans.

What does it cost?

We don't publish a price list, because every event is different. After a free call you get a written plan with the price within 24 hours. Venue and vendor costs are quoted openly.

Can events run without the other services?

Yes. Events can run on their own. They work harder beside search, brand and lifecycle, because the invite list, the follow up and the recap all travel through them. You can see how all four fit together on our B2B SaaS marketing page.
