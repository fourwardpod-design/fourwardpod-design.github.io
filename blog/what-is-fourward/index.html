What is Fourward?

Fourward is a marketing team for B2B SaaS companies. Instead of separate agencies for SEO, PR, email and events, a software company works with one senior team that plans all of it together and leads the work.

What Fourward does

Everything a B2B SaaS marketing department does, in four areas that run on one plan and one weekly cadence.

Search, content and AI visibility

SEO and technical SEO, GEO and AEO so that ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews cite you, content strategy, comparison pages and hubs, website and conversion work, migrations and schema, and analytics from first visit to pipeline. Our GEO guide for B2B SaaS shows how we think about AI search.

Brand, PR and partnerships

Positioning and brand, PR and media coverage in the trade press buyers read, founder thought leadership, online reputation management on G2, Trustpilot and Glassdoor, LinkedIn organic marketing, social media, partnerships and award entries.

Lifecycle and demand generation

HubSpot and CRM set-up, nurture and trigger email workflows, newsletters, webinars and podcasts, account-based marketing, sales enablement and funnel reporting from first touch to closed-won.

Field marketing and events

Owned event series, executive dinners and roundtables, partner events, paid media to fill the room, event content, and follow-up workflows so every attendee reaches sales within the week.

Who Fourward works with

Fourward works only with B2B SaaS companies. We're a good fit when:

  • You don't have a marketing team yet, or have one or two people who need senior support.
  • You need several channels moving at once, such as search, PR and events around a launch, and don't want to manage separate agencies.
  • Buyers compare you in AI answers, and you're missing from them or described wrongly.
  • Marketing has to report pipeline, not just traffic or impressions.

How working with Fourward starts

From first call to first day of work, in four steps. You decide at each one.

  1. Discovery call. Thirty minutes with the Fourward team. Tell us the number that has to move and what has been tried.
  2. Free audit. An SEO, AEO and GEO audit: what ranks, what AI says about you and where you lose visibility.
  3. Written plan. Priorities, timeline, price and what we need from you, sent within three working days.
  4. Kickoff. Once the plan works for you, we set up access, agree the first week and get started.
