What a rank tracking tool does

A keyword is the phrase a buyer types into Google, such as "payroll software for startups". A rank tracker searches each of your keywords on a schedule and records where your pages appear. It then charts the changes and flags drops.

Most rank trackers report the same handful of things:

  • PositionYour page's place in the results for one keyword, where 1 is the top organic result.
  • VisibilityOne score that blends your positions across all tracked keywords, so you can see the overall trend.
  • SERP featuresExtra boxes on the SERP (search engine results page), such as AI Overviews, maps and videos, and whether you appear in them.
  • CompetitorsThe same positions for rival sites you add, so you can tell a market shift from your own drop.

The "ten blue links" are Google's classic list of plain results, and rank trackers were built to watch them. The newer question is whether you also appear in AI answers: Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.

Four things that separate rank trackers

Most rank trackers look alike on their homepages. The differences show up on the pricing page, in four places.

How often it checks

Daily checks show a drop the day after it happens. Weekly checks cost less per keyword, but a problem can sit unseen for up to a week. Ahrefs, for example, says Rank Tracker updates weekly by default on every plan.

How many keywords you get

Every plan caps the number of keywords. Some caps assume weekly checks: Advanced Web Ranking says the keyword counts on its pricing page use its default weekly update. Pick your list before you pick a plan, and our guide to keyword research tools helps with that.

Locations and devices

Google shows different results in different cities and on different devices. Local tracking matters if you sell in set regions or run events in set cities. Semrush offers ZIP (postal) code level data, Mangools tracks at city level and Nightwatch checks daily in any location.

AI answers

AI answers don't have a fixed position like the blue links. Trackers handle them in two ways. Some show whether an AI Overview appears for your keyword. Others ask AI engines a set of prompts, the questions a buyer might type, and record whether you're named.

Key takeaway

Price per keyword only means something at the same refresh speed. Compare daily plans with daily plans, and weekly plans with weekly plans.

I didn't create accounts or run tests, so nothing here claims hands-on results. Prices are as listed on 25 November 2026. AccuRanker and Nightwatch list their prices in euros.

1. Semrush

Best for Teams that want rank tracking, AI answer tracking and keyword research in one SEO suite.

Semrush is an SEO suite, and Position Tracking is its rank tracker. Semrush says the tool pulls fresh data every day, with local data down to ZIP code level.

Its FAQ answers the AI question directly: "Yes, Semrush tracks AI Overviews and prompts in LLMs like ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, and Gemini." LLMs (large language models) are the systems behind AI assistants like ChatGPT.

  • Price$139 a month, or $117.33 a month billed annually, for the entry SEO plan. Pricing page
  • Keywords500 on the entry plan.
  • RefreshDaily, with ZIP code level locations.
  • AI answersAI Overviews, plus prompts in ChatGPT, AI Mode and Gemini.

My take The safest all-round pick if you want one login for keywords, ranks and AI answers. It is also the most expensive per keyword in this list, so large sites will need a bigger plan.

2. SE Ranking

Best for Small teams that want many keywords checked daily without a top-tier suite price.

SE Ranking is an SEO suite that includes rank tracking. Its Core plan checks 2,000 keywords every day. That is four times Semrush's entry allowance, for $10 less a month.

Core also lists AI visibility tracking as part of the plan. Check the prompt allowance on the pricing page before you rely on it for AI answers.

  • Price$129 a month, or $103.20 a month billed annually, for Core. Pricing page
  • Keywords2,000 on Core.
  • RefreshDaily.
  • AI answersAI visibility tracking, listed on the Core plan.

My take The most daily keywords per dollar in this list, at about $6.45 per 100 keywords. A strong default for a B2B site that wants daily data without paying suite prices.

3. Ahrefs

Best for Teams that already pay for Ahrefs and can work with weekly checks.

Ahrefs is an SEO suite, and Rank Tracker is one of its tools. The Lite plan includes 750 tracked keywords. Ahrefs' help centre says Rank Tracker's default update frequency is weekly on every plan.

Lite also lists tracked AI prompts as a separate line, for following AI answers rather than blue links.

  • Price$129 a month for Lite. Pricing page
  • Keywords750 on Lite.
  • RefreshWeekly by default.
  • AI answersTracked AI prompts, listed on Lite.

My take Fine if Ahrefs is already your SEO tool, because ranks sit next to the rest of your data. Weekly checks are slow around a launch, so ask Ahrefs what daily updates cost before you rely on it.

4. AccuRanker

Best for SEO teams and agencies that track thousands of keywords and want them checked daily.

AccuRanker is a rank tracker for SEO teams. Its pricing page promises "daily, reliable keyword updates", and the Professional plan starts at €229 a month for 2,000 keywords.

For AI answers, AccuRanker has AccuLLM. AccuRanker says it shows how your brand performs in ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews and AI Mode, including your average rank across tracked prompts. The AccuLLM page shows no price.

  • Price€229 a month for Professional. Pricing page
  • Keywords2,000 on Professional.
  • RefreshDaily.
  • AI answersAccuLLM covers ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews and AI Mode.

My take It costs more than SE Ranking for the same 2,000 daily keywords. It suits teams for whom rank tracking is the main job. Ask whether AccuLLM is included before you count it in the price.

5. Advanced Web Ranking

Best for Large sites and agencies that want the most keywords per dollar and review rankings weekly.

Advanced Web Ranking (AWR) is a rank tracker. Its Pro plan is listed at $139 a month for 7,000 keywords. AWR says those counts assume its default weekly update, so daily checks use up the plan faster.

Pro also includes AI visibility tracking, AI citations and mentions. Citations are the links an AI answer shows as its sources.

  • Price$139 a month for Pro. Pricing page
  • Keywords7,000 on Pro, at weekly checks.
  • RefreshWeekly by default.
  • AI answersAI visibility tracking, AI citations and mentions.

My take About $1.99 per 100 keywords at weekly checks, the lowest rate in this list. If you need daily data, ask AWR how many keywords Pro covers at that speed.

6. Nightwatch

Best for Small teams that want daily checks and AI answers at the lowest listed entry price here.

Nightwatch sells rank tracking and AI visibility in the same plans. Its Starter plan is €79 a month for 500 keywords, "checked daily, in any location".

The plans also count AI responses for the prompts you track. The plan table lists ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Mode and AI Overview as AI platforms.

  • Price€79 a month for Starter. Pricing page

My take The cheapest way into daily tracking with AI answers in this list. 500 keywords fits a focused B2B site, not a large content library.

Which rank trackers track AI answers

Every tracker in this list now mentions AI search. What each one covers differs a lot, so read the detail, not the label.

  • Inside rank trackingSemrush tracks AI Overviews and prompts in ChatGPT, AI Mode and Gemini. Nightwatch counts AI responses for your prompts, starting on Starter.
  • As a named productAccuRanker's AccuLLM covers ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews and AI Mode. Mangools has AI Search Watcher as a separate tool.
  • Listed on the planSE Ranking Core lists AI visibility tracking, Ahrefs Lite lists tracked AI prompts, and Advanced Web Ranking Pro lists AI visibility, citations and mentions.

Tracking AI answers is a different job from tracking blue links. You track prompts, not keywords, and an answer can name you without linking to you. If AI answers matter more to you than Google positions, compare dedicated tools in our guide to the best AI visibility tools.

Mistakes to avoid when buying a rank tracker

These five mistakes are easy to avoid before you sign up.

  1. Tracking every keyword you rank for. Track the terms that bring buyers. A short list you act on beats a long list nobody reads.
  2. Comparing daily and weekly plans as equals. A weekly keyword costs less to check. Compare daily plans with daily plans.
  3. Forgetting locations and devices. Ask whether one keyword on mobile and desktop, or in two cities, uses one slot or two.
  4. Treating an AI label as AI tracking. Check which engines are covered and how many prompts you get before you pay.
  5. Watching ranks without reporting them. Send a short monthly report to the people who own the pages. Our list of SEO reporting tools covers that part.

FAQs

What is the best rank tracking tool?

Semrush is the best all-round pick if you want ranks and AI answers in one suite. SE Ranking gives the most daily keywords for the money. Advanced Web Ranking gives the most keywords on weekly checks.

How often should a rank tracker update?

Daily, if you publish often, run launches or watch a site after a Google update. Weekly is enough for a small site that reviews rankings once a week. Ahrefs, Advanced Web Ranking and Mangools Basic default to weekly.

How much does a rank tracker cost?

Listed entry plans in this guide run from €79 a month (Nightwatch, 500 daily keywords) to €229 (AccuRanker, 2,000 daily keywords). Semrush, SE Ranking, Ahrefs and Advanced Web Ranking sit between $129 and $139 a month.

Can rank trackers track AI Overviews and ChatGPT?

Some can. Semrush tracks AI Overviews and prompts in ChatGPT, AI Mode and Gemini, and AccuRanker's AccuLLM covers ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews and AI Mode. Check the engines and prompt limits on each plan.

Why does my tracker show a different position than I see in Google?

Google changes results by location and device. A tracker checks from a fixed location and device, so your own search can differ. Trust the tracker's trend over one manual check.

Is a rank tracker the same as an AI visibility tool?

No. A rank tracker follows keywords in search results. An AI visibility tool follows prompts in AI answers and records whether your brand is named, and several trackers now do both.
