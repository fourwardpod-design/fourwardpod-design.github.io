What a SaaS link building agency does

A link building agency gets other websites to link to yours. For a SaaS company, the goal is links from sites your buyers read, pointing to the pages you want to rank.

You will see the same few terms in every proposal:

  • BacklinkA link from another website to yours.
  • DRDomain Rating, a score from Ahrefs for how strong a site's own backlinks are. Higher means stronger.
  • OutreachEmailing editors and site owners to ask for a link or a mention.
  • Guest postAn article you write for another site, with a link back to yours.
  • Niche editA link added to an article that another site has already published.
  • Digital PRPitching stories and data to journalists, so their articles mention and link to you.
  • PlacementOne article or link the agency gets for you.

Links are one part of search. Our guide to SEO for B2B SaaS covers the other steps, so you know what links should support.

Key takeaway

Ask every agency the same first question: how is each link earned, and is anyone paid for it?

Questions to ask before you sign

  1. How is each link earned? Outreach, digital PR, guest posts and niche edits carry different risks and costs.
  2. Is any site paid for a link? If yes, ask how that link is marked, since Google's rules treat unmarked paid links as spam.
  3. Can I see sample sites? Check that your buyers read them, not just their DR.
  4. Which of my pages will the links point to? Links help most on the pages you need to rank.
  5. What will the monthly report show? Ask for every live link, the site, the page it points to and the date.

For a wider list, see our questions to ask a marketing agency before you sign any contract.

Mistakes to avoid when hiring a link building agency

Google's spam rules target links made "primarily for the purpose of manipulating search rankings" and call them link spam. Most hiring mistakes come back to that rule, or to judging links by count.

  • Buying unmarked paid linksGoogle's examples of link spam include paying for links, or for posts that contain links. Paid links are fine only when they are "qualified", which means tagged in the page code as paid.
  • Counting links, not sitesA link from a site your buyers never visit does little for you. Judge each link by who reads the site.
  • Hiring before your pages are readyuSERP lists "pages worth ranking" among the things it looks for in a client. Fix the pages links will point to first.
  • Ignoring mentionsIn my view, a brand mention on a site buyers read is worth having even without a link.
  • No list of live linksWithout a monthly list of every link, you cannot check the work or spot links that vanish.

FAQs

What is the best SaaS link building agency?

It depends on your budget and stage. For links planned with your search and AI visibility work, Fourward. For SaaS-only agencies, dofollow.com or MADX Digital, and for mid-market and enterprise SaaS, uSERP.

How much does a SaaS link building agency cost?

Published monthly prices here start at $2,500 (MADX Digital), $3,700 (Page One Power) and $5,000 (uSERP). Siege Media starts at about $12,000 to $15,000 a month. Per-link services start at $100 a placement (Respona) and $375 a link (Editorial.Link).

Is it safe to pay an agency for backlinks?

Paying an agency for its work is normal. Paying a site for a link is link spam under Google's rules, unless the link is marked as paid.

What is the difference between link building and digital PR?

Link building gets links directly, through outreach, guest posts or placements. Digital PR pitches stories and data to journalists, and the links come with the articles they choose to write.

How do I check the quality of an agency's links?

Ask for a list of sample sites and check whether your buyers read them. A backlink tool such as Ahrefs, which we use at Fourward, shows each site's DR and traffic.
