SaaS link building: the tactics that work, what to skip and how to budget

A practical guide to earning links SaaS buyers trust: six tactics that work, the ones Google's spam policy rules out, and published prices to budget with.

SaaS link building is the work of getting other websites to link to a software company's site, so Google and buyers trust it more. The links that work in 2026 come from sources buyers already read: original data, expert quotes in the press, integration partners and fair comparison posts. Unlabelled paid links, link swaps and mass directory listings break Google's spam rules, so skip them.

This guide covers six tactics that work, the ones to skip, what links cost on published price pages, and how to budget for each stage.

I lead search, content and AI visibility at Fourward, and our team also does PR and partnerships work, so I have a stake in this topic. Every rule and price below comes from the publisher's own page, checked on 8 November 2026.

Key takeaways
  • SaaS link building is earning links from other websites to a software company's site, ideally from sites its buyers read and trust.
  • Google says it uses links to work out what pages are about, and that PageRank is still part of its core ranking systems.
  • Google's spam policy lists buying links, excessive link swaps and low-quality directory links as link spam.
  • Published prices run from $350 a link to $15,000 a month or more. BuzzStream's survey average is $597 a link.
  • Mentions matter too. Ahrefs found brand mentions track AI Overview visibility far more closely than backlinks do.

What SaaS link building is, and why it still matters

A backlink is a link from another website to yours. SaaS link building is the planned work of earning those links for a software product, usually to the pages buyers search for.

Why Google still counts links

Google's ranking systems guide says it has "various systems that understand how pages link to each other as a way to determine what pages are about". PageRank, one of Google's link analysis systems, "continues to be part of our core ranking systems".

Why mentions matter for AI answers

Ahrefs, an SEO tool company, studied 75,000 brands to see what goes with being named in AI Overviews, the AI summaries Google shows above its results. Brand web mentions scored 0.664 and backlinks scored 0.218, on a correlation scale where higher means a closer match.

Ahrefs itself warns that correlation is not causation, so read this as a pattern, not proof. A link on a site your buyers read can count twice: once as a link and once as a mention. Links are one of nine steps in our SEO guide for B2B SaaS.

Six link building tactics that work for SaaS

Each tactic below earns a link because the other site gains something real: data, a quote, an integration or a fair comparison.

1. Publish original data

Your product sees patterns nobody else can see, such as how teams use a feature or how long a task takes. Turn one of those patterns into a short study with a clear headline number, a chart and a note on method.

Pitch the headline finding to journalists and bloggers who cover your buyers' industry. Update the study each year, so every new edition gives writers a fresh reason to link.

2. Answer journalists as an expert

Journalists post requests for expert sources on matching services. Qwoted describes itself as "Connecting the Media with Expert Sources" and offers a free account.

  • Answer only where you have real experience. A founder or senior team member with a clear view is the best source.
  • Put one fact in every answer. A short, specific answer is easier to quote than a general opinion.

3. Get listed by your integration partners

If your product connects to other tools, ask each partner to list you in its app directory or integrations page. Those links come from sites your buyers already use.

Keep the list honest. Google's spam policy names "partner pages exclusively for the sake of cross-linking" as link spam, so list only integrations that work.

4. Get into comparison posts and best-of lists

Buyers read "best tool for this job" lists and side-by-side comparisons while they choose. Our guide on how to get cited by ChatGPT explains why these pages matter for AI answers too.

  • Send facts the author can check. Say who the product is for, what it costs, and include one screenshot.
  • Watch for pay-to-be-listed offers. Google asks sites to mark "advertisements or paid placements" with a sponsored label.

5. Write guest posts for sites your buyers read

A guest post is an article you write for another website. Pick sites your buyers read, write something they can use, and link to the page that helps the reader most.

Keep the anchor text (the clickable words of a link) natural, such as your brand name or the page title. Paying for a post that contains a link is on Google's link spam list.

6. Turn unlinked mentions into links

Some articles already name your product without linking to it. Set a web alert for your brand name, then ask each writer to link to the page they described. This is the quickest link to ask for, because the site already chose to write about you.

Link building tactics SaaS companies should skip

Google's spam policy defines link spam as "creating links to or from a site primarily for the purpose of manipulating search rankings". These examples from its list matter most for SaaS sites:

  • Paid links. The policy lists "Exchanging money for links, or posts that contain links".
  • Link swaps. "Excessive link exchanges" are on the list, which means trading links mainly to trade links.
  • Mass directory listings. Google names "Low-quality directory or bookmark site links".
  • Automated link services. The policy lists "Using automated programs or services to create links to your site".

Paid placements are allowed when they are labelled. Google says such links are fine "as long as they are qualified". Its link guide explains the label: rel="sponsored", a tag in the link's code that tells Google the link was paid for.

How to budget for SaaS link building

Pick the model that fits your cash, your team's time and how many links you need.

Small budget: do it in-house

Start with the tactics that cost time, not cash: expert answers, partner listings and unlinked mentions. Your main cash cost is a backlink tool, such as Ahrefs Lite at $129 a month. I use Ahrefs in my work.

Our post on in-house marketing vs an agency compares the full costs of each route for B2B SaaS.

Mid budget: pay per link, with care

Per-link services cost $350 to $375 a link on Editorial.Link's price page. Before you pay any service, ask how each link is earned, and ask to see sample sites. If the service pays the other site for an unlabelled link, Google's policy calls that link spam.

Larger budget: a digital PR retainer

A retainer is a fixed monthly fee. BuzzStream's survey average is about $5,458 a month, and Siege Media's guide runs from $5,000 to $15,000 or more. Our list of the best digital PR agencies compares eight, with their published prices.

Key takeaway

Budget for links on sites your buyers read. A few links from trade sites in your category are worth more than many links from sites your market never visits.

How to tell if SaaS link building is working

Links only help AI answers if AI engines can read your site first. Fourward's free AI visibility check runs 30 checks on what AI engines can read about a site.

  1. Count the relevant sites. Each month, list the new sites that link to you, and mark the ones your buyers read.
  2. Check the pages that got links. Track their Google rankings for the searches each page targets.
  3. Follow the visits. In Google Analytics, check referral visits and demo requests from the linking sites.
  4. Ask AI tools your buyers' questions. Note whether ChatGPT and Google's AI answers name you, and which sites they cite.

FAQs

What is SaaS link building?

SaaS link building is earning links from other websites to a software company's site. The links that help most come from sites buyers read, such as trade press, partner directories and comparison posts.

How much does SaaS link building cost?

Published per-link prices start around $350 to $375 a link. BuzzStream's survey puts the digital PR average at $597 a link. Siege Media estimates $5,000 to $15,000 or more a month for agency work.

Is it safe to buy links for a SaaS website?

Not if the link counts toward rankings. Google's spam policy lists exchanging money for links as link spam. Paid placements are fine when they carry the rel="sponsored" label.

How many backlinks does a SaaS site need?

No published number exists. Google uses links to understand what pages are about. In my view, a few relevant links to the right pages beat a large count of unrelated ones.

Do backlinks help with AI search?

They help, but mentions seem to matter more. Ahrefs found brand mentions scored 0.664 against AI Overview visibility, while backlinks scored 0.218. Ahrefs warns this shows a pattern, not cause.
