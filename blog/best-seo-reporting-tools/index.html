What an SEO reporting tool does

An SEO reporting tool collects data from places like Google Search Console, Google Analytics and your SEO software. It turns that data into a report that someone else can read. Most tools send the report on a schedule, as a PDF (a fixed file), a link or a login.

The tools differ less in the data they pull and more in who they are built for. Two readers matter most.

A board report answers one question: is search paying off?

  • Business numbers first. Leads, demos or pipeline from organic search come before traffic and rankings.
  • The same few numbers each time. A board reads trends, so the metrics should not change every quarter.
  • A comparison point. Each number sits next to last quarter, last year or a target.
  • What happens next. The report ends with the plan and what it needs, such as budget or people.

A client report answers another: what did we get for the fee?

  • Your brand, not the vendor's. White label means the report carries your agency's logo and colors, with no sign of the software that made it.
  • Work done next to results. Clients want to see the pages published and links earned, not only the charts.
  • Access at any time. A client portal or a shared link saves you from emailing files every week.
  • Notes in plain words. A short summary explains what changed and why.
Key takeaway

Decide who reads the report and what they decide with it. That choice narrows the list faster than any feature table.

1. Google Data Studio

Best for In-house teams that want a free leadership dashboard and have someone who can build it.

Data Studio is Google's free report builder. It was called Looker Studio until April 2026, when Google returned it to its original name. Google describes it as a no-cost tool for shareable, customizable dashboards and reports.

You connect data sources and design every page yourself. One limit matters for search: our guide to AI Overviews data in Search Console explains why dashboards built on Google's API can't split AI impressions out.

  • What it buildsLive dashboards and reports, laid out by you.
  • Made forBoard and leadership updates built in-house.
  • PricingFree. Google also documents a Pro edition, Data Studio Pro.
  • Your brandingYou design the whole report, so it carries your brand.

My take The best free start for a board dashboard. For an agency with many clients, the build time for each client adds up quickly.

2. Semrush My Reports

Best for Teams already on Semrush that want scheduled reports with little set-up.

Semrush builds reports from its own data in a feature called My Reports. Scheduled reports update and go out on a monthly, weekly or daily basis. Pricing is per report, which keeps the cost easy to predict.

  • What it buildsReports from 20+ Semrush tools, with PDF export and scheduled sending.
  • Made forClient reports and monthly team updates.
  • PricingA Base report is $10 a month. A Pro report is $20 a month.
  • Your brandingBranding and white-labeling come with the Pro report only.

My take Easy to justify if your team already works in Semrush every day. Ten Pro reports cost $200 a month, so compare that with a per-client platform before you grow.

3. SE Ranking

Best for Small agencies that want rank tracking and branded client reports on one bill.

SE Ranking is an SEO suite with a built-in Report Builder. The number of reports depends on your plan. Branded reporting is sold as an add-on called the Agency Pack.

  • What it buildsReport Builder reports: 10 on the Core plan, 30 on Growth.
  • Made forClient reports for small agencies and consultants.
  • PricingCore is $103.20 a month, billed yearly. Pricing page
  • Your brandingThe Agency Pack costs $69 a month more, billed yearly only. It adds white label, plus unlimited scheduled reports and AI summaries.

My take Good value when the SEO data and the client report should live in one tool. Budget for the Agency Pack, because white label and unlimited scheduling sit there.

4. Ahrefs Report Builder

Best for Teams on Ahrefs that want custom SEO reports for clients or leadership.

Ahrefs says its reporting lets you create custom SEO reports and show your site's performance to clients or teams. You share the results as PDFs. If you are still choosing between the two big suites, our Semrush vs Ahrefs comparison covers data and pricing.

  • What it buildsCustom SEO reports from Ahrefs data, shared as PDFs.
  • Made forClients and internal teams.
  • PricingLite is $129 a month. Report Builder is listed as an add-on with 50 reports and scheduling. Pricing page
  • Your brandingNot stated on the pages I checked.

My take A natural fit if Ahrefs is already your main SEO tool. Price the add-on before you commit, because the plan price alone is not the reporting price.

Mistakes that get SEO reports ignored

A good tool can still send a report nobody reads. These five mistakes are the usual cause.

  1. Leading with rankings for a board. A board decides on money and people. Put leads or pipeline from search first, and rankings underneath as the reason.
  2. Too many charts. If a report needs a call to explain it, cut it down to the numbers that drive a decision.
  3. Numbers with nothing to compare. A figure means little alone. Show it next to last quarter, last year or the target.
  4. Changing the metrics every month. Readers learn a report's shape. Keep the same numbers in the same places so the trend is easy to see.
  5. No next step. End every report with what happens next and who owns it. A report without actions reads like a status update.
Key takeaway

A plain one-page report that a board reads beats a polished dashboard nobody opens.

FAQs

What is the best SEO reporting tool?

For a free board dashboard, Google Data Studio. For agencies, AgencyAnalytics has the clearest per-client pricing at $20 a client. If you already pay for Semrush, SE Ranking or Ahrefs, start with its built-in reports.

Is there a free SEO reporting tool?

Yes. Google Data Studio, called Looker Studio until April 2026, is free, but you build the report yourself. Fourward's free AI visibility check is also free, though it checks a site rather than building reports.

What should an SEO report for a board include?

A few business numbers first, such as leads or pipeline from organic search, then the traffic and rankings behind them. Compare each with last quarter or a target, and end with next steps.

What is a white-label SEO report?

It is a report that carries your agency's logo and colors instead of the software vendor's. AgencyAnalytics, DashThis and Whatagraph include it on their entry plans. Semrush, SE Ranking and Databox charge extra for it.

How much do SEO reporting tools cost?

Google Data Studio is free. Paid tools start at $10 a report (Semrush), $20 a client (AgencyAnalytics) or $44 a month paid yearly (DashThis). Flat plans run to $399 a month (Databox) and from €699 a month billed yearly (Whatagraph).
