The best Ahrefs alternative depends on the job you need done. Semrush is the closest full suite for keyword data, and Majestic covers backlinks alone. SE Ranking or AccuRanker handle rank tracking, and Google Search Console is free if price is the problem.

Key takeaways
  • An Ahrefs alternative is an SEO (search engine optimization) tool that does one of Ahrefs' main jobs, such as keyword research, backlinks or rank tracking.
  • Ahrefs Lite costs $129 a month for 1 user, 750 tracked keywords and 6 months of historical data. Compare every alternative against those three limits.
  • SE Ranking's Core plan tracks 2,000 keywords daily at the same $129 list price. Semrush's SEO plan tracks 500 for $139.
  • For backlinks alone, Majestic Lite costs $49.99 a month. For your own site's search data, Google Search Console is free.

What Ahrefs costs, and the four jobs people leave it for

Ahrefs is a full SEO suite, which means one tool for keyword research, backlinks, rank tracking and site audits. Its pricing page lists four paid plans and a custom Enterprise plan:

  • Starter, $29 a month: the pricing page lists no limits for this plan.
  • Lite, $129 a month: 1 user included, 750 tracked keywords, 1,000 credits per user as a usage allowance, and 6 months of historical data. You can add 2 more users at $40 a month each.
  • Standard, $249 a month: 2,000 tracked keywords and 2 years of historical data.
  • Advanced, $449 a month: 5,000 tracked keywords. Paying yearly saves up to 17%.

Name the job before you compare tools. Ahrefs does four main jobs, and each job has its own alternatives:

  • Keyword data: what people search for, and how hard each term is to rank for. Look at Semrush.
  • Backlinks: which sites link to you and to your competitors. Look at Majestic.
  • Rank tracking: where your pages rank each day for the keywords you choose. Look at SE Ranking and AccuRanker.
  • Price: paying less, or nothing, for the data you actually use. Look at Google Search Console.
Key takeaway

Ahrefs Lite gives one user 750 tracked keywords and 6 months of history for $129 a month. If one of those limits is your problem, a specialist for that one job may cost less.

1. Semrush

Best for Teams that want to keep one suite for keyword data, competitor research and rank tracking.

Semrush is the other big all-in-one SEO suite. Its entry SEO plan is pitched "for freelancers and small businesses looking to grow their online visibility with SEO". Its Starter plan adds AI search tracking on top.

  • PricingThe SEO plan is $139 a month, or $117.33 a month billed annually. Starter, which includes 300 AI visibility reports per day, lists $199 a month.
  • Tracking500 keywords tracked daily across 5 websites on the SEO plan.
  • Next to Ahrefs Lite$10 a month more on monthly billing, and 250 fewer tracked keywords.
  • Free option"Try Semrush free for seven days. Cancel anytime."

My take Semrush is the closest swap if you want one suite. It costs a little more than Ahrefs Lite, so switch for fit, not savings. Our Semrush vs Ahrefs comparison goes through the two suites job by job.

2. SE Ranking

Best for Teams that need more tracked keywords for the same money as Ahrefs Lite.

SE Ranking is an SEO suite that covers research and daily rank tracking. Its Core plan lists "Unlimited keyword, competitor, backlink research", so one plan covers keyword data, backlinks and rank tracking.

  • PricingCore lists $129 a month, or $103.20 a month on annual billing, which saves 20%. Growth lists $279 a month.
  • Tracking2,000 keywords tracked daily on Core, and 5,000 on Growth.
  • SeatsCore includes 1 manager seat.
  • Free optionA 14-day free trial, with no credit card required.

My take At the same $129 list price as Ahrefs Lite, Core tracks 2,000 keywords a day instead of 750. If rank tracking is your main job, start your comparison here.

3. Majestic

Best for Link builders and PR teams who only need backlink data.

Majestic is a backlink tool. Its plans are built around two link indexes: the Fresh Index on Lite, and the Historic Index added on Pro. An index here means Majestic's stored map of which pages link to which.

  • PricingLite is $49.99 a month, or $499.99 a year. Pro is $99.99 a month and adds the Historic Index.
  • Next to Ahrefs LiteLite costs $79.01 a month less than Ahrefs Lite, less than half the price.
  • Free optionNo free trial is listed. "Lite and Pro plans have a 7-day money-back guarantee for new customers."

My take If links are the only report you open, Majestic is the cheapest paid pick here. Check that you won't miss Ahrefs' keyword and tracking tools first. Our guide to SaaS link building covers what to do with the data.

4. AccuRanker

Best for Teams whose main report is rankings and who want a tool built for that job.

AccuRanker is a dedicated rank tracker. Its pricing page promises "daily, reliable keyword updates" on the keywords you choose, and its plans scale up to Enterprise.

  • PricingProfessional starts at €229 a month. Paying yearly saves up to 15%.
  • TrackingDaily updates are listed as a Professional plan feature.
  • UsersEnterprise includes unlimited users and domains. The page doesn't state user limits for Professional.
  • Free optionThe pricing page doesn't mention a free trial.

My take AccuRanker costs more than Ahrefs Lite, so it isn't the price pick. Before you compare, check how many keywords the €229 plan covers, because that sets your real cost per keyword.

5. Google Search Console

Best for Anyone leaving Ahrefs on price who mainly needs data on their own site.

Google Search Console is Google's own tool for site owners. In Google's words, "Google Search Console is a free service offered by Google". Its Performance report "shows important metrics about how your site performs in Google Search results".

  • JobGoogle Search traffic data for your site, and which sites link to it. It covers your own site, not your competitors'.
  • PricingFree.
  • Also freeAhrefs Webmaster Tools gives "free website analytics, technical audit & SEO metrics for your verified websites."

My take Search Console data comes straight from Google, but only for your site. Pair it with one paid specialist from this list, and you may not need a full suite. Our guide to AI Overviews data in Search Console shows what else it reports.

6. Fourward AI visibility check

Best for Teams that want to know, for free, what AI engines can read about their site.

SEO tools measure where you rank on Google. A newer job is checking what AI engines, such as ChatGPT and Gemini, can read about your site. An engine that can't read your site has little to say about you in an answer.

  • JobChecking what AI engines can read about your site.
  • What it checks75 checks on what AI engines can read about a site.
  • PricingFree.
  • Not forKeyword data, backlinks or rank tracking. It sits next to whichever SEO tool you pick.

My take Run it before you pay for an AI tracking add-on from any suite. Run the free check, and see our guide to the best AI visibility tools for paid trackers.

Questions I'd ask before switching

  1. Which job do I pay Ahrefs for? List the reports your team opens each week. Buy for those, not for the rest.
  2. How many keywords do I track? Compare your count with 750 on Ahrefs Lite and with each alternative's daily limit.
  3. How many people need a login? Count seats, because extra users change the monthly price.
  4. Monthly or yearly? Compare every price on the same billing term.
Key takeaway

Match the tool to the one job you pay for, then check keywords, seats and billing term. A specialist plus Search Console can cost less than a second suite.

Mistakes to avoid when you switch from Ahrefs

Each of these mistakes costs money or data when you switch tools.

  • Comparing list prices, not limits. Semrush's SEO plan costs $10 more than Ahrefs Lite and tracks 250 fewer keywords. SE Ranking Core costs the same and tracks 1,250 more.
  • Paying for two suites. If you move to Semrush or SE Ranking, cancel Ahrefs rather than keeping both.
  • Losing your history. Ahrefs Lite keeps 6 months of historical data. Save the reports you need before you cancel, because a new tracker starts its history from the day you add keywords.
  • Judging backlink tools by the biggest total. Test each tool on links you already know about, and see which ones it finds.
  • Leaving before checking cheaper Ahrefs options. Ahrefs Starter costs $29 a month, and Ahrefs Webmaster Tools is free for sites you verify. If price is the only problem, check those first.
  • Skipping the trial. Use SE Ranking's 14-day trial or Semrush's seven-day trial on your own keywords, not on sample data.

FAQs

What is the best free alternative to Ahrefs?

Google Search Console is free from Google, and it shows search traffic data for your own site and which sites link to it. Ahrefs Webmaster Tools is also free for websites you verify.

Is Semrush better than Ahrefs?

Neither wins every job. Semrush's SEO plan costs $139 a month and tracks 500 keywords daily, against $129 and 750 tracked keywords on Ahrefs Lite. Pick the suite that fits the job you need.

What is the cheapest Ahrefs alternative?

Google Search Console is free for your own site. Among the paid tools here, Majestic Lite is the cheapest at $49.99 a month for backlink data. Ahrefs' own Starter plan costs $29 a month.

Which Ahrefs alternative is best for backlinks?

Majestic is built for backlinks, with the Fresh Index on Lite and the Historic Index on Pro at $99.99 a month. SE Ranking's Core plan also includes unlimited backlink research for $129 a month.

Which Ahrefs alternative is best for rank tracking?

SE Ranking's Core plan tracks 2,000 keywords daily for $129 a month, against 750 on Ahrefs Lite. AccuRanker is a dedicated rank tracker with daily updates, from €229 a month.

Does Ahrefs have a free plan?

Ahrefs Webmaster Tools is free for websites you verify, and its Site Audit checks for 170+ common technical and on-page SEO issues. Paid plans start with Starter at $29 a month.
