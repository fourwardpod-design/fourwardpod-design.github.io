What a digital PR agency does

Digital PR (public relations) is press work aimed at websites instead of print. The agency finds a story, pitches it to journalists and editors, then reports the articles that mention or link to you.

You will see the same few terms in every proposal:

  • PlacementAn article on another site that mentions or links to you.
  • DRDomain Rating, a score from Ahrefs for how strong a site's own backlinks are. Higher means stronger.
  • RetainerA fixed monthly fee for ongoing work.
  • Pay per placementYou pay for each article or link you get, not for the hours worked.

Link building is a narrower job. A link-building service sells links, which can include guest posts and listicle spots. A digital PR agency earns coverage that a journalist chose to write.

Key takeaway

Ask every agency the same question: which sites will mention us, and do our buyers read them?

How much a digital PR agency costs

Agencies price digital PR in three ways: per link, as a monthly package, or as a larger retainer. The table above shows which model each agency uses.

For a market view, BuzzStream surveyed agency leaders and freelancers. Its report was last updated on 12 June 2026. It puts the average monthly contract at about $5,458. Most retainers fall under $10,000 a month, and half are below $5,000. The average cost per link was $597.

Siege Media's cost guide, last updated in August 2024, explains the spread. It says digital PR can start at $5,000 a month for a strong story, and passes $15,000 when new content must be made. For other marketing services, see our SaaS marketing agency pricing guide.

Key takeaway

Divide the price by the placements you would actually want. A cheap link on a site your buyers never read is not cheap.

Mistakes to avoid when hiring a digital PR agency

These five choices waste the most PR budget.

  1. Counting links, not readers. Ask for a list of target sites and check that your buyers read them.
  2. Calling paid placements PR. Paid guest posts and listicle spots can bring links, but no journalist chose to write them.
  3. Hiring without a story. Siege Media's guide says cost rises when new content must be made. Bring data, a product angle or an expert.
  4. Not asking what the price covers. Content, reporting and contract length change the real cost. Our questions to ask a marketing agency help.
  5. Ignoring AI answers. Check which sites ChatGPT and Google's AI answers cite for your category, then ask the agency to target them.

FAQs

What is the best digital PR agency for B2B SaaS?

It depends on budget and goal. For PR run with search and AI visibility, start with Fourward. For large data-led campaigns, look at Siege Media or Fractl.

How much does a digital PR agency cost?

Published prices here run from $375 a link (Editorial.Link) to about $15,000 a month (Siege Media). BuzzStream's survey puts the average monthly contract at about $5,458.

Is digital PR the same as link building?

No. Digital PR earns coverage that journalists choose to write, and links often come with it. Link building gets links directly, sometimes through paid guest posts.

Should I pay per link or a monthly retainer?

Pay per link when you need a few links on sites you already know. A retainer suits campaigns built over several months.

Does digital PR help my brand show up in AI answers?

It can, because AI answers often cite other websites, not just yours. Fourward's free AI visibility check shows what AI engines can read about you today.
