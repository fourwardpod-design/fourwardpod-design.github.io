Google AI Mode's new features this week, and what B2B SaaS teams should adjust in search and paid plans

Google added booking links for US home services to AI Mode, put ads into follow-up questions and started a core update. Here is what changed, what didn't, and what B2B SaaS teams should adjust.

The newest Google AI Mode features are direct booking links for US home services, announced on 4 November 2026, and ads that can now show in follow-up questions. Google also started its November 2026 core update on 10 November. None of the three changes what Google says a page needs to appear in AI Mode.

This post explains each change, what is not new, and what B2B SaaS teams should adjust in their search and paid plans this month.

Key takeaways
  • Google AI Mode is a separate mode in Google Search where people ask longer questions and follow-ups, and get an AI answer with links to websites.
  • On 4 November 2026, Google added direct booking links for home services in AI Mode, in the US only, through four partner platforms.
  • In the US, ads can now appear across follow-up questions in AI Mode, and you can't opt out of them separately from Search.
  • The November 2026 core update started on 10 November. Wait until it finishes before you judge any ranking change.
  • Google still says there are no extra requirements to appear in AI Mode. Clear pages that Google can index decide who gets linked.

What are the new booking links in AI Mode?

On 4 November 2026, Robby Stein of Google announced the change on X. He wrote that Google is "making it even easier to research & book home services with direct links in AI Mode."

How the booking links work

  • Partners. Google is working with Angi, Thumbtack, Housecall Pro and Jobber, and will add ServiceTitan soon. These are platforms that home service businesses use to take bookings and find jobs.
  • Where. Search Engine Roundtable reports that the feature is available in the United States only for now.
  • What the customer does. Housecall Pro says homeowners "can book an appointment directly from the search experience."
  • Cost to the business. Housecall Pro's announcement says requests come "with no associated lead fees or bidding." It also says its integration is coming soon to eligible US customers.

What Google hasn't said

Search Engine Journal notes that Stein's post doesn't say how Google picks which businesses to show. I also found nothing from Google on whether booking links will reach other countries or other kinds of business.

Why it matters if you sell software

This is my view, not Google's. The booking links let people act inside AI Mode through partner platforms, not only on a business's own website. If Google brings the same idea to software, the platforms that list your product could matter more for demos and trials.

How do ads in AI Mode follow-up questions work?

Multiturn ads are ads that can appear in the follow-up questions of an AI Mode conversation, not only after the first question. On 28 October 2026, Google said it is "launching multiturn ads in the U.S." to allow ads in follow-up queries.

Google Ads Help now confirms it. The help page says ads can appear "directly within or alongside these AI-generated responses (including across follow-up questions in AI Mode)" when a search has commercial relevance.

What you can and can't control

  • Eligibility is automatic. Existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are automatically eligible to show ads in AI answers.
  • No separate opt-out. You can't target ads only to AI Overviews or AI Mode, or opt out of them separately from Search.
  • No separate report. Queries that trigger your ads in AI Mode are included "in aggregate" in your search terms report. You can't see which searches were AI Mode follow-ups.

I listed this year's earlier ad changes, including the expansion of ads in AI Mode to new markets, in my post on Google updates in 2026.

Does the November 2026 core update affect AI Mode?

A core update is a broad change to how Google ranks pages in Search. Google's Search Status Dashboard lists the "November 2026 core update" with a start date of 10 November 2026.

I found nothing from Google on whether this update changes AI Mode answers. Google does say a page must be "indexed and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet" to be linked in AI Mode. In my view, that means a big ranking change in normal results can also show up in AI Mode.

Key takeaway

Two of this week's changes are about AI Mode itself, and one is about Google rankings in general. For most B2B SaaS teams, the ad change and the core update matter now. The booking links don't apply to software yet.

Which AI Mode features are not new this week?

Some features in recent news are older. Here is what started before November.

  • Notebooks in AI Mode. Notebooks are saved spaces for a research project inside AI Mode. Chrome Unboxed reported in August 2026 that they were rolling out in English across more than 180 countries, excluding the European Economic Area (EEA).
  • Notebooks need a personal account. Google's help page says you must be signed in to "a personal Google Account that you manage on your own." That suggests buyers using a work account can't use them yet.
  • More links in answers. Google's October changes added more inline links and tested a section for official sites. I covered them in Google's AI Mode links update.

Google's AI features guide is also unchanged. It still says no additional requirements or special optimizations are needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. It adds that AI Mode may use query fan-out, which means running several related searches behind one question.

What B2B SaaS teams should adjust in search

These steps are my advice, based on what Google has published this month. Start with the first two this week.

  1. Mark 10 November in your reports. Note the core update start in your analytics and Search Console notes. Hold big site changes until the dashboard shows the update as complete, so you can tell its effect from yours.
  2. Give every buyer action one clear page. Demo, free trial, pricing, login and support each need one current page on your own domain. The booking links suggest Google wants AI Mode to help people finish tasks, so make yours easy to find.
  3. Keep your product listings current. The booking links run through partner platforms. Check that marketplaces, integration directories and review profiles show your current plans, features and logo.
  4. Answer the follow-up questions. Query fan-out means AI Mode runs related searches behind each question. Pages on pricing, integrations, security and alternatives give it more to quote.
  5. Measure what you can. I explained where to find Google's AI answer data in my guide to AI Overviews data in Search Console. Fourward's free AI visibility check runs 30 checks on what AI engines can read about your site.

What B2B SaaS teams should adjust in paid search

Your Search and Performance Max campaigns can already show in AI Mode follow-ups. These steps are my advice for the next few weeks.

  1. Read your search terms report every week. AI Mode queries are mixed in with your normal queries. Look for long, question-style searches, and add negative keywords for ones that never lead to a qualified lead.
  2. Match landing pages to follow-up questions. A buyer who asks "how much does it cost for 50 users" should land on pricing, not the homepage. Check that each ad group points to the page that answers its question.
  3. Don't move budget on one week of data. Google announced multiturn ads on 28 October and started the core update on 10 November. Changes in cost or lead volume may come from either one.
  4. Judge results by lead quality, not placement. Google doesn't split out AI Mode results, so compare demo and trial quality before and after, by campaign.

Mistakes to avoid this month

  • Reading the core update too early. The update only started on 10 November. Wait for Google to mark it complete on its dashboard before you compare.
  • Treating the booking links as a B2B feature. They cover US home services through four partners. I found nothing from Google about bringing them to software.
  • Expecting an AI Mode report in Google Ads. Google reports these queries in aggregate, so plan your measurement around total campaign results.
  • Paying for special AI Mode markup. Google says no special optimizations are needed. An indexed page that answers the question clearly is the requirement.

FAQs

What are the new features in Google AI Mode this week?

On 4 November 2026, Google added direct booking links for US home services in AI Mode, through Angi, Thumbtack, Housecall Pro and Jobber. Google Ads Help also now says ads can appear across follow-up questions in AI Mode.

Can B2B SaaS companies use the AI Mode booking links?

No. The booking links cover home services in the US, through four partner platforms. I found nothing from Google about extending them to software.

Can I stop my ads from showing in AI Mode follow-up questions?

Not separately from Search. Google Ads Help says you can't target ads only to AI Mode or opt out of it separately from Search.

Does the November 2026 core update change AI Mode?

Google hasn't said. AI Mode links to pages that are indexed and eligible to show with a snippet, so big ranking changes may show there too. Wait for the update to finish before you judge.

Do I need special markup to appear in AI Mode?

No. Google says there are no additional requirements or special optimizations to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. The page must be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet.
