ChatGPT's new update: business analytics, citations and what to change

OpenAI is reportedly building analytics that show how often ChatGPT names a business and sends it clicks. Here is what the tool shows, what it leaves out and what to do now.

The newest ChatGPT update for businesses is a tool OpenAI hasn't announced yet. On 6 November 2026, Search Engine Roundtable reported that OpenAI is building analytics for businesses inside ChatGPT. The tool would show a verified business how often ChatGPT names it in answers and how often people click through to its website.

This post covers what the tool shows, what it leaves out, how it fits with ChatGPT citations and ads, and what B2B SaaS marketers should change now.

Key takeaways
  • ChatGPT business analytics is a reported OpenAI tool that counts how often ChatGPT mentions a business and how often people click through to its site.
  • It is reportedly part of a larger Business Hub and only open to businesses that verify ownership.
  • It reportedly shows no prompts, no user data and no competitor ranking, so it can't tell you why your numbers change.
  • OpenAI has given no launch date and no price. You can prepare now by letting its search crawler in and tracking your own mentions.

What the new ChatGPT business analytics tool is

Juozas Kaziukėnas spotted the tool and shared a screenshot in a LinkedIn post. The screen invites a business to "Track how often your business is mentioned in ChatGPT conversations". Search Engine Roundtable calls it a tool for analytics and citations. Kaziukėnas says it "measures both impressions and clicks".

Impressions: how often ChatGPT names you

An impression here means one ChatGPT answer that names your business. Kaziukėnas describes it as the "number of times a business appeared in ChatGPT responses". Think of it as a mention count, much like impressions in Google Search Console.

Clicks: how often people visit your site

A click is a visit to your website from a link inside a ChatGPT answer. Search Engine Roundtable says the tool "tracks those events daily and shows them as a chart".

Business info and products

Search Engine Roundtable lists a second feature: "Show customers what you offer: Add your latest products and keep your business info up to date". For a SaaS company, I'd expect that to mean plans, features and integrations. That's my guess, because nobody has published the fields yet.

Who can use it, and when

Access is reportedly limited. Kaziukėnas says the tool is "part of a larger Business Hub feature and only available to business that have verified their ownership". Nobody has published how a SaaS company would verify ownership.

  • Launch date: none. Kaziukėnas, quoted by Search Engine Roundtable, says "The feature is a work in progress and I saw its first steps as early as August".
  • Official word: I found no OpenAI announcement or help page for the tool on 8 November 2026.
  • Price: no price has been reported.
  • Ads: Search Engine Roundtable says it "seems to be separate from ChatGPT ads and more designed for organic ChatGPT responses".

Organic responses are the normal answers ChatGPT writes, as opposed to paid placements. If you're weighing the paid side, I covered whether ChatGPT ads are worth it for B2B SaaS.

What it means for ChatGPT citations

Three terms get mixed up here. A mention is when ChatGPT names your brand in an answer. A citation is a link to a page the answer used as a source. A click is when a reader follows that link to your site.

The reported tool counts mentions and clicks. It would give businesses numbers from OpenAI itself, instead of estimates from outside tools.

What you still won't know

Kaziukėnas is clear about the limits: "Obviously it doesn't show prompts, user data, or any kind of competition benchmark or ranking". In practice, the tool won't tell you three things:

  • Which buyer questions led ChatGPT to name you.
  • Which competitors it named instead of you.
  • Why your mentions went up or down in a given week.

Nobody has reported whether clicks will be broken down by page. You will still need your own prompt tracking to explain the numbers.

Key takeaway

The tool would count how often ChatGPT names you and sends you visits. It won't explain why, so it adds to your tracking rather than replacing it.

Other ChatGPT updates this autumn

One official change this autumn was a new model. GPT-6 is OpenAI's newest GPT (generative pre-trained transformer) model, the kind of AI behind ChatGPT. An announcement on OpenAI's developer forum says GPT-6 began rolling out on 7 October 2026 for Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise. Free and Go users followed from 8 October.

The same post says GPT-6 can build answers from text, visuals and interactive elements. A new model can change which sources ChatGPT picks. So I'd recheck your most important buyer questions after any model change.

What B2B SaaS marketers should change now

The tool isn't live, but both of its numbers depend on work you can do now. The steps below are my advice, based on OpenAI's crawler documentation and the reported features.

  1. Let OpenAI's search crawler in. OpenAI says "OAI-SearchBot is used to surface websites in search results in ChatGPT's search features". Check that your robots.txt file and firewall allow it. OpenAI says robots.txt changes can take about 24 hours to reach its search results. Fourward's free AI crawler checker tests 20 AI crawlers against both.
  2. Don't confuse it with GPTBot. OpenAI says GPTBot is used to make its "generative AI foundation models more useful and safe". Blocking GPTBot is a training choice. Sites that opt out of OAI-SearchBot "will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers," OpenAI says, though they can still appear as navigational links.
  3. State product facts in plain sentences. Put pricing, plans, integrations and who the product is for on your own pages, in text. ChatGPT can only cite what it can read. My guide on how to get cited by ChatGPT covers this step by step.
  4. Start a baseline now. Pick a fixed list of the questions your buyers ask, and check which brands ChatGPT names each month. An AI visibility tool can run this on a schedule and show competitors, which the reported tool won't.
  5. Track ChatGPT visits in your analytics. Set up a report for visits referred by chatgpt.com. When OpenAI's click count arrives, you can compare it with your own data.
  6. Pick an owner for your business listing. Access will reportedly need verified ownership. Decide now who on your team will claim the listing and keep your product names and descriptions the same everywhere you control them.

For the wider set of steps, from crawler access to mentions on other sites, see our GEO guide for B2B SaaS. GEO (generative engine optimization) means getting your product named in AI answers.

Mistakes to avoid

A screenshot of an unreleased tool invites quick reactions. These are the ones I'd avoid:

  • Treating it as launched. OpenAI hasn't announced the tool, and the features could change before it ships.
  • Waiting for it to start measuring. A baseline built from your own prompt checks shows change over time. Without one, the first chart has nothing to compare against.
  • Reading a count as a cause. A drop in mentions won't come with a reason. Check your prompts, your crawler access and your competitors before you change pages.
  • Blocking the wrong crawler. Blocking OAI-SearchBot keeps your site out of ChatGPT search answers, so the tool would have fewer mentions and clicks to count.

FAQs

What is the new ChatGPT update for businesses?

OpenAI is reportedly building a business analytics tool in ChatGPT. It would show a verified business how often ChatGPT mentions it and how often people click through to its site, as a daily chart. OpenAI hasn't announced it.

Is the ChatGPT business analytics tool available yet?

No. Juozas Kaziukėnas, who found it, calls it a work in progress whose first steps appeared as early as August. There is no launch date and no OpenAI help page for it yet.

Will it show which prompts mention my brand?

No. According to the LinkedIn post that revealed it, the tool doesn't show prompts, user data or any competitor benchmark or ranking. You'll need your own prompt tracking for that.

Is it the same as ChatGPT ads?

No. Search Engine Roundtable says it seems separate from ChatGPT ads and designed for organic answers, the normal responses ChatGPT writes.

How do I make sure ChatGPT can cite my site?

Allow OAI-SearchBot in your robots.txt file and at your firewall. OpenAI uses it to show websites in ChatGPT search, and says robots.txt changes can take about 24 hours to apply.

Will the ChatGPT business tool cost anything?

Nobody knows yet. No price has been reported, and OpenAI hasn't said anything about the tool.
