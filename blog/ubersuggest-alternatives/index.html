Key takeaways
  • An Ubersuggest alternative is a keyword research, rank tracking or site audit tool that does some or all of Ubersuggest's job, often with higher limits.
  • Ubersuggest costs $29, $49 or $99 a month. The $29 Individual plan allows 150 searches a day, 125 tracked keywords and 1,000 page scans, for 1 project.
  • SE Ranking and Serpstat publish much bigger audit allowances: 250,000 pages a month on SE Ranking Core and 30,000 pages to audit on Serpstat Individual.
  • For audits alone, you may not need to pay. Ahrefs Webmaster Tools is free for sites you verify, and Screaming Frog is free up to 500 URLs.

What Ubersuggest costs, and which limits a growing team hits first

Ubersuggest is a low-cost SEO tool for keyword research, rank tracking and site audits. Its pricing page lists three plans. Each has a 7-day free trial on monthly billing.

  • Individual, $29 a month or $290 lifetime: 1 project, 150 searches a day, 125 tracked keywords per project and 1,000 page scans per project.
  • Business, $49 a month: the middle plan, with more projects and higher limits than Individual.
  • Enterprise/Agency, $99 a month or $990 lifetime: 15 projects, 900 searches a day, 300 tracked keywords per project and 10,000 page scans per project.

Prices are in US dollars, and Ubersuggest says it serves localized pricing based on the visitor's region. Three terms explain the limits:

  • Project: one website you set up in the tool.
  • Tracked keyword: a search term whose Google ranking the tool checks for you on a schedule.
  • Page scan: one page the site audit crawls, which means the tool visits the page and checks it for problems.

Where a SaaS site outgrows the plan

  • Page scans: a SaaS site with a blog, help docs and integration pages can pass 1,000 pages. Past that point, the Individual plan's audit can't cover the whole site.
  • Tracked keywords: 125 per project covers your product terms. Comparison pages, use-case pages and blog posts each add more terms to watch.
  • Projects: a second website, such as a new product site, needs a higher plan.

Ubersuggest has added AI tracking too. Its pricing page describes a metric that tracks how a brand appears across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

Key takeaway

Ubersuggest is cheap to start, but its caps are counted per project and are small next to the tools below. For a growing SaaS site, check the page scan and tracked keyword limits first.

Questions to answer before you switch

  1. How many pages does your site have? Count blog posts, help docs and landing pages, then compare the total with each audit limit.
  2. How many keywords must you track? List your product, comparison and use-case terms, then check them against each plan.
  3. How often do you need rankings checked? Weekly checks are fine for trends, and they stretch a check budget like Serpstat's further.
  4. Who will act on the findings? An audit report only helps if someone owns the fixes. Our guide to SEO for B2B SaaS covers what to do with the data.
Key takeaway

Size the plan to your page count, keyword list and check frequency. Pick a plan with spare capacity, so you only switch once.

Mistakes to avoid when you switch from Ubersuggest

Check for these five mistakes before you move your data to a new tool.

  • Comparing numbers with different units. A per-project cap, a monthly cap and a check budget are not the same. Convert each one to your own site size first.
  • Buying a lifetime plan to save money. A lifetime plan fixes the price of a tier, not its limits. If you expect to grow, price the plan you will need next year.
  • Paying for data you can get free. For sites you own, Ahrefs Webmaster Tools and Google Search Console cover a lot. Our guide to the best keyword research tools compares free and paid options.
  • Checking only Google. Ubersuggest and Semrush both now sell AI search tracking. Ask any new tool which AI engines it covers.
  • Cancelling before you export. Export your rank history and audit reports from Ubersuggest first, because a new tool starts tracking from its first day.
Key takeaway

Compare limits in the same units, start with the free audit tools and keep your old data before you cancel.

FAQs

What is the best free alternative to Ubersuggest?

For site audits, Ahrefs Webmaster Tools is free for websites you verify, and Screaming Frog's free version crawls up to 500 URLs. To see why AI engines can't read your site, Fourward's AI visibility check is free.

How much does Ubersuggest cost?

Ubersuggest's pricing page lists Individual at $29 a month, Business at $49 and Enterprise/Agency at $99, in US dollars. Lifetime plans cost $290 to $990, and prices can vary by region.

Is SE Ranking better value than Ubersuggest?

For a growing team, often yes. SE Ranking Core costs $103.20 a month at the yearly rate, with 2,000 keywords tracked daily and 250,000 audit pages a month. Ubersuggest's $99 plan lists 300 tracked keywords and 10,000 page scans per project.

Which Ubersuggest alternative is best for site audits?

Screaming Frog is built for crawling, with a free version up to 500 URLs and a £199 yearly licence. Among suites, SE Ranking Core lists 250,000 audit pages a month and Serpstat Individual 30,000 pages to audit.

Does Ubersuggest track AI search?

Yes. Its pricing page describes a metric that tracks how a brand appears in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. Semrush also sells combined SEO and AI search plans.
