Questions about search and AI visibility.

What is the difference between SEO, GEO and AEO?

SEO gets your pages ranked in Google. GEO and AEO get your brand named and cited inside AI answers, in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews. The work overlaps, so we plan them together: pages AI can read, answers it can quote and mentions on the sites it trusts. Our guide to GEO for B2B SaaS explains the steps.

How long does it take to see results?

It depends on where you start. Technical fixes and crawler access can show up quickly. Rankings for competitive terms build over months. Your written plan says what to expect and when, based on your audit.

Do you write the content, or do we?

We do. Research, writing, design and publishing are part of the work. If you have writers in house, we plan and edit with them.

How do you measure AI visibility?

We check which buyer questions AI engines answer with your brand, which pages they cite and where competitors are named instead. It is reported each month beside rankings and pipeline. You can start with our free AI visibility check.

Can you work with the website and CMS we have?

Yes. We work in the stack you already use, and we handle migrations when a move is the right call.

What does it cost?

We don't publish a price list, because the work depends on the plan. After a free call and audit, you get a written plan with the price within 24 hours, as a monthly partnership or a fixed project.

Can search run without the other services?

Yes. Search can run on its own. It works harder with brand, lifecycle and events beside it, because mentions, emails and events all feed what Google and AI engines see. You can see how all four fit together on our B2B SaaS marketing page.
