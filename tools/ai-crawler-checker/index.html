AI search bots vs AI training bots

Not all AI bots do the same job. Search and answer bots fetch pages so tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Copilot can cite them. Block these and AI answers can't quote you. Training bots collect pages to train future models. Blocking them is a business choice and doesn't remove you from today's AI answers.

How to allow AI search bots in robots.txt

Add rules like these near the top of your robots.txt file. Each one lets a bot read your whole site.

User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
Allow: /

User-agent: PerplexityBot
Allow: /

User-agent: Claude-SearchBot
Allow: /

To keep a training bot out while staying in AI search, block it by name, for example User-agent: GPTBot followed by Disallow: /.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI crawler?

An AI crawler is a bot that reads web pages for an AI company. Some, like OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot, fetch pages so AI answers can cite them. Others, like GPTBot and ClaudeBot, collect pages to train AI models.

Does blocking GPTBot remove my site from ChatGPT?

Not on its own. OpenAI uses GPTBot for training and OAI-SearchBot for ChatGPT search. You can block GPTBot and still allow OAI-SearchBot, so ChatGPT search can find and cite your pages.

My robots.txt allows AI bots. Why are they still blocked?

Many firewalls and CDNs block AI bots before they reach robots.txt, for example a "block AI bots" setting. The firewall test in this checker loads your homepage as AI bots to spot that.

What is llms.txt?

llms.txt is a short text file at the root of your site that lists your most useful pages for AI tools. It is optional, and Google has said it does not use it, but some AI tools read it.

Should I block AI training bots?

It is a business choice. Blocking training bots keeps your content out of future model training. It does not stop AI search bots, which are the ones that decide whether you get cited today.
