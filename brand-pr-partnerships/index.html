Questions about brand and PR.

Do you guarantee press coverage?

No honest team can. What we promise is a steady flow of stories journalists can use, pitched to the right people, and a clear report of what landed.

We have no big news. Can PR still work?

Yes. Data from your product, a clear point of view and founder bylines earn coverage without a funding round or a launch.

How do you handle reviews?

We set up and complete your profiles, ask customers at the right moments and answer every review in your voice. We never write or buy reviews.

Who writes for our founders?

We do, with them. A short call each month gives us their views and stories, and nothing goes out without their approval.

How does PR help with AI search?

AI engines describe you using what other sites say. Coverage, reviews and mentions on trusted sites make it more likely they name you, and name you correctly. Our guide to GEO for B2B SaaS covers how mentions feed AI answers.

What does it cost?

We don't publish a price list, because the work depends on the plan. After a free call and audit, you get a written plan with the price within 24 hours, as a monthly partnership or a fixed project.

Can brand and PR run without the other services?

Yes. Brand and PR can run on their own. They work harder beside search, lifecycle and events, because every mention you earn also feeds what Google and AI engines say. You can see how all four fit together on our B2B SaaS marketing page.
