    Questions SaaS teams ask us.

    What does a B2B SaaS marketing team do?

    It runs the work a marketing department does for a software company: search and AI visibility, content, brand and PR, lifecycle email and demand generation, and events. Fourward does all of it as one senior team, on one plan, measured in pipeline.

    What is included?

    SEO and technical SEO, GEO and AEO, content and comparison pages, website and CRO, positioning and PR, online reputation management, LinkedIn and social media, HubSpot and email workflows, webinars, ABM, sales enablement, event series, executive dinners, partner events and paid media for events. Your plan picks what matters for your goal.

    How much does it cost?

    We don't publish a price list, because the work depends on the plan. After a free call and audit, you get a written plan with the price within three working days, as a monthly marketing partnership or a fixed-scope project.

    How do you report results?

    Against pipeline. You get a baseline report before the work starts, a weekly progress update, a review call every two weeks and a monthly full report.

    Do you help with AI search, like ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews?

    Yes. GEO and AEO are part of the search work: crawler access, pages AI engines quote, and mentions on the sites they cite. Our guide to GEO for B2B SaaS explains the steps.

    Do you only work with B2B SaaS companies?

    Yes. Every page, plan and programme we run is built for software companies that sell to other businesses.

    How do we get started?

    Book a 30-minute call. We look at your site, your rankings and what AI says about you before we join, then send a written plan with the price within three working days.

    Got a number that won't move?

    Signups, demos, pipeline, AI citations, share of voice. Send us the metric and the deadline, and we'll come back with a plan.
